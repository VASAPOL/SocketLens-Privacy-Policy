# SocketLens Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 21, 2026**

SocketLens is a network debugging app for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## Information Stored on Your Device

SocketLens stores connection presets, server settings, message drafts, and saved messages locally on your device. These may include hostnames, IP addresses, ports, URLs, and message contents.

Traffic logs are held temporarily in memory and are not automatically uploaded to the developer. Your device’s backup services may include locally saved app data, depending on your settings.

## Network Communications

SocketLens connects to endpoints you configure and accepts incoming connections when you start a server. Messages and connection information are exchanged with those endpoints to provide the debugging features you use.

The operators of those endpoints may receive and retain your IP address and transmitted data. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

## Data Collection and Tracking

SocketLens does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

The app does not automatically send your presets, message contents, or traffic logs to the developer. We do not sell your personal information.

## Local Network Permission

SocketLens requests Local Network access to communicate with nearby devices and run debugging servers. You can change this permission in your device’s Settings or System Settings.

## Exporting and Sharing

You may choose to export traffic logs using the system share interface. Exported logs can contain message contents, endpoint information, and timestamps.

Review logs before sharing. Files you export and copies held by recipients are outside the app’s control.

## Retention and Deletion

Saved presets and messages remain on your device until you remove them. You can delete connection presets and saved messages within the app and clear displayed traffic logs.

Traffic logs are not retained across app process restarts unless you export them. Exported files and device backups must be managed separately through the relevant app or backup service.

## Security

SocketLens uses the operating system’s app storage protections. It is a debugging tool, not a secure credential vault.

Network traffic may be unencrypted depending on the protocol and connection settings you use. Avoid storing or transmitting sensitive information unless appropriate for your testing environment.
